About Burger Hut

Founded by Kavya Priya, Burger Hut began as a small passion project to serve high-quality, fresh, and mouth-watering burgers to the local community. With a dream to revolutionize the burger experience, Kavya Priya crafted a unique menu focusing on taste, creativity, and premium ingredients.

In 2020, Burger Hut officially opened its doors to the public. Despite challenges, it quickly gained a loyal fan base. Thanks to innovative recipes and a cozy dark-themed atmosphere, the restaurant stood out from traditional burger joints. The year 2021 marked a turning point, as Burger Hut's fame skyrocketed through social media buzz and food bloggers' recommendations.

Today, Burger Hut is celebrated not just for its delicious burgers but also for its commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. Expanding to multiple locations, it continues to win hearts with every bite. We believe in creating memorable experiences — one burger at a time.
